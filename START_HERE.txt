IMPORTANT EXTRA NASA DATA
The extra sources are documented but their original raw/reduced data files were not uploaded or downloaded into this pack. Prioritize PSI-60 for SPICE soot targets, PSI-62 for BASS/BASS-II derived spread targets, PSI-98 for SAFFIRE-I imagery/sensors, and PSI-101 for SAME-R detection evidence. LUCI now has an identified NASA NTRS publication describing simulated lunar-gravity combustion results; this is external NASA evidence, not a local PSI CSV. FM² has a NASA project page but no dataset bundled here. Do not fabricate files or count derived analyses as independent flight tests.
