# Additional NASA source to monitor: FM² — Flammability of Materials on the Moon

**Category:** Lunar-gravity solid-material flammability.  
**Official NASA project page:** https://www.nasa.gov/glenn/glenn-expertise-space-exploration/physical-sciences-program/combustion-science/flammability-of-materials-on-the-moon-fm2/  
**Page status checked:** NASA page updated 28-Apr-2026.  
**Bundle status:** Project information only; no FM² raw or analysed dataset is included.

NASA describes FM² as investigating material combustion at approximately one-sixth lunar gravity, with four fuel samples in a sealed chamber under atmospheric conditions planned for future lunar habitats/vehicles. Cameras record flame size, intensity, color and spread rate; sensors measure flame/fuel temperatures, oxygen and carbon dioxide.

## Why it matters

FM² is directly aligned with the dashboard's lunar habitat and pressurized rover use cases. It may eventually provide a direct lunar-gravity material-flammability bridge beyond microgravity and Earth-gravity evidence.

## Data to ingest only after release

- Gravity level and time history.
- Pressure, oxygen and carbon dioxide conditions.
- Calibrated flame-front/spread-rate imagery and uncertainty.
- Flame/fuel temperature, intensity and color measurements.
- Sample/run identifiers, hardware metadata and test protocol.

The NASA project page is not a dataset. Do not create synthetic FM² test rows, imply that data has been downloaded, or treat project plans/description as measured results. Add a dataset record only when an official file package or publication with extractable, traceable measurements is available.
