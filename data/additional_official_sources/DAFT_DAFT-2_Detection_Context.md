# Additional sources: DAFT and DAFT-2 — dust/aerosol measurement feasibility

**Category:** Smoke detector context, dust discrimination and background aerosol characterization.  
**NASA background reference:** https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/iss_technical_publication_030116.pdf  
**Bundle status:** NASA background source reviewed; original DAFT/DAFT-2 measurement files are not included. Verify exact PSI investigation identifiers and file inventory before acquisition.

DAFT examined particle/dust and air-quality measurements aboard the ISS, including the ability of instruments to measure airborne particles. This is relevant because a spacecraft fire detector must distinguish combustion smoke from background dust and other aerosols.

## Data to retrieve

- Background particle size distribution and number concentration over time.
- Dust / non-combustion aerosol type and release timing.
- Instrument make/model, measurement principle, calibration and detection limits.
- Sensor location, airflow, pressure and temperature.
- Raw instrument signal and quality/contamination flags.

## Restrictions

Use DAFT/DAFT-2 to describe background aerosol and sensor qualification. Do not label dust particles as combustion smoke, and do not use DAFT data as smoke-generation labels without paired combustion-source context.
