# Additional source: PSI-60 — Computational and Experimental Analysis of SPICE Data Sets

**Category:** Soot / smoke-point analysis.  
**Official NASA PSI repository search:** https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/search?data_source=psi&data_type=investigation  
**PSI investigation identifier shown in NASA catalogue:** PSI-60  
**Bundle status:** Catalogue metadata reviewed; no PSI-60 source files are included in this pack. Open the NASA PSI catalogue and search the exact investigation title to access its current record/files.

## Why this is the highest-priority addition for SPICE

NASA's PSI catalogue describes this as a ground investigation analysing SPICE datasets using image processing, experimental diagnostics and detailed soot modelling. The catalogue summary indicates derived distributions and soot-volume-fraction information from PSI-107 SPICE data.

## Data to retrieve

- Analysed soot volume-fraction profiles / distributions.
- Image-derived flame and luminous-flame geometry/length.
- Image-to-test/run mapping back to PSI-107 identifiers.
- Camera, lens, exposure, calibration, spectral/channel and image-processing metadata.
- Uncertainty, quality flags and measurement definitions.
- Model inputs/outputs or diagnostics documented by the investigators.

## Model use and restrictions

Use PSI-60 to identify validated SPICE target columns and connect them to the PSI-107 input conditions. PSI-60 is a derived analysis of SPICE data, not a separate independent flight-test cohort. Never add its analysed rows to PSI-107 and count the same flame twice. The output targets still need physical definitions and an auditable link to their originating experiment.
