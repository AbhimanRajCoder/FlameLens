# Additional external NASA source: LUCI — Lunar Combustion Investigation

**Category:** Partial-gravity / simulated lunar-gravity solid-fuel flammability.  
**NASA Technical Reports Server record:** https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20250010653  
**NASA flight-opportunity overview:** https://www.nasa.gov/stmd-flight-opportunities/flight-summaries/lunar-gravity-simulation-via-suborbital-rocket/  
**Bundle status:** NASA publication/overview identified; the original tabular/raw measurement package is not included in this handoff.

## Why this is a major addition

NASA's NTRS summary for *Lunar Combustion Investigation: Flammability Results from a Rotating Sounding Rocket* reports extended-duration simulated lunar-gravity combustion tests using centrifugal acceleration on a rotating suborbital vehicle. The summary describes approximately 11 RPM rotation, an effective radius around 1.22 m, two samples (cotton/fiberglass composite fabric and a 4-mm PMMA rod), flame imaging from two orthogonal cameras, oxygen measurement and burns lasting more than 2.5 minutes. It reports determination of flame spread/growth rates and an oxygen-related extinction limit.

NASA's Flight Opportunities page describes LUCI as recording flame velocity images, radiant emissions, temperatures and product-gas concentrations at a variety of gravity accelerations.

## Required data to extract / obtain

- Gravity acceleration/time history and uncertainty (do not assume a perfectly constant 1/6 g from mission label alone).
- Sample identity, dimensions, orientation, material composition and conditioning.
- Ambient pressure, oxygen concentration and gas composition.
- Airflow / relative flow conditions and vehicle rotation state.
- Calibrated flame-front position versus time from both camera views.
- Flame spread and growth rates with measurement interval and uncertainty.
- Radiant emission, flame/fuel temperatures and product-gas concentrations.
- Test/run IDs and the mapping between publication plots, image files and raw test records.

## Model use

- Flame-spread research: the highest-priority partial-gravity bridge evidence; compare with microgravity BASS/SAFFIRE and normal-gravity baselines only after harmonizing material, geometry, oxygen, flow, gravity and measurement definitions.
- Extinction research: use the reported oxygen-related extinction boundary as a separate solid-material / partial-gravity evidence target, not as a droplet extinction label.
- Investigation ranking: highly relevant to lunar surface habitat and pressurized rover material flammability.

## Restrictions

1. LUCI results are external NASA publication evidence in this pack, not a newly supplied PSI dataset. Do not place it under the PSI raw source categories until a verified original data package and provenance are obtained.
2. It represents simulated lunar gravity via centrifugal acceleration, not an experiment conducted on the lunar surface.
3. Do not merge its values with BASS/SAFFIRE or FLEX until target, material, geometry, flow, oxygen and gravity definitions are aligned.
4. If only figure-digitized values are available, label them as digitized-from-publication with pixel/digitization uncertainty; do not present them as original machine-readable sensor data.
