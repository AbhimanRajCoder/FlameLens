# Additional source: PSI-98 — SAFFIRE-I

**Category:** Flame spread / solid-fuel flammability; also fire growth and smoke-transport evidence.  
**Official NASA PSI record:** https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-98  
**DOI:** 10.60555/0t15-1z43  
**Bundle status:** Official source metadata reviewed; raw/analysed files are not included in this pack.

## Why add it

SAFFIRE-I is directly relevant to the NASA dashboard's large-scale spacecraft fire-prevention question. NASA describes a large SIBAL composite fabric sample, approximately 0.94 m × 0.4 m, with airflow around 20 cm/s. The investigation used two cameras, thermocouples, radiometers, pressure and gas-composition sensing (O2/CO2), and anemometers.

## Data products to retrieve

- Original and processed flame image/video sequences.
- Flame-front and pyrolysis-front position versus time, with calibration and timebase.
- Flame length / flame-growth measures.
- Temperature, pressure, oxygen, carbon dioxide and airflow streams.
- Radiometer measurements / radiative heat-release context.
- Sample material and geometry metadata, operating conditions, sensor layout and engineering reports.

## Model use

- Flame-spread track: potential image-derived flame spread/growth targets after calibration and interval definitions are verified.
- Detection track: fire/smoke transport and environmental sensor evidence where actual smoke measurements are available.
- Investigation ranking: high relevance to prevention and large-scale fire growth in a spacecraft cargo volume.

Do not count SAFFIRE-I frames as independent experiments. Group all time-series frames by burn/test. Do not assume all listed PSI file categories contain the specific reduced measurements above; inspect the file inventory before mapping fields.
