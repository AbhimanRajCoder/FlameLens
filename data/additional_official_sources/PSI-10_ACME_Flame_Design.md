# Additional source: PSI-10 — ACME / Flame Design

**Category:** Adjacent soot-inception and gaseous spherical diffusion-flame evidence.  
**NASA PSI record:** https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-10  
**NASA overview:** https://science.nasa.gov/biological-physical/investigations/flame-design/  
**Bundle status:** NASA catalogue/overview reviewed; source files are not included.

NASA describes Flame Design as a study of soot production/control in spherical gaseous-fuel flames, with interest in soot-inception limits and oxygen-enriched combustion. It is relevant to fire-safety science and soot physics but has a different physical geometry and target from SPICE co-flow jet smoke-point measurements.

## Data to retrieve

- Fuel identity/composition and reactant stream composition.
- Flow rates, pressure, inert dilution and flame configuration.
- Soot-inception-limit measurements and exact threshold definitions.
- Temperature and flame-structure measurements, where available.
- Source run IDs, diagnostic methods, calibration and uncertainty.

## Restrictions

Keep it as a separate gaseous spherical-flame branch or supporting evidence. Do not merge its soot-inception target with SPICE smoke-point length or FLEX droplet extinction labels.
